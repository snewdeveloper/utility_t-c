Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026  ·  Last updated 9 November 2026

This policy explains what the Folio Android app does with your information. It is written to describe what the app actually does, not the widest set of things a policy could permit. Every claim below corresponds to something in the app’s source.

Folio is published by SkIndie Dev (“we”, “us”), Tamil Nadu, India. Contact: snewdeveloper@gmail.com

At a glance

  • Your documents never leave your phone. Merging, splitting, signing, scanning, text recognition and every other tool run on the device. We do not upload your files, and we cannot see them.
  • There is no Folio server and no account. No sign-up, no login, no user profile. We do not know who you are.
  • We collect nothing about you. The app ships no analytics provider and no crash reporter.
  • Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. Where consent is required, the app asks before requesting any ad.
  • Text recognition runs on your device. Pages you scan are not sent anywhere for processing.
  • We do not sell your data. There is no data to sell.

1Information stored on your device

Everything in this section stays on your phone. None of it is transmitted to us.

WhatWhere it lives
Documents you open, create or editRead and written on the device. Results are saved to Documents/Folio, or a folder you choose.
Your recent files listA local database holding each file’s name, size, page count, when you last opened it, whether you starred it, and the page you had reached.
Your settingsTheme, whether you have seen the welcome tour, and a count of completed jobs used to decide when to ask you for a review.
Signatures you drawSaved privately inside the app’s own storage so you can reuse one without drawing it again.
Text extracted for search, read aloud and compareHeld in memory while the screen is open and never written anywhere.

Clearing the app’s data removes the recent files list, your settings and your saved signatures. Uninstalling removes all of it, except documents you deliberately saved to shared storage, which remain on your phone as ordinary files.

2Information we collect about you

None. Nothing is sent to us, because there is nowhere to send it: Folio ships no analytics provider and no crash reporter, and we operate no server.

A small set of anonymous events — a tool was opened, a job finished, a purchase completed — is defined in the code and written to the device log on development builds only. These never leave the device and are not present in the version you install from Google Play.

If this ever changes, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before the release that changes it.

3Advertising (Google AdMob)

Folio is free, and the free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. Ads appear as a banner on list screens and occasionally between jobs. They are never placed over a document you are reading or editing.

AdMob may use an advertising identifier and coarse device and usage data to select and measure ads. That processing is Google’s, under Google’s privacy policy; we receive no personal data from it. Your documents are never shared with AdMob.

Where consent is legally required, the app asks for it through Google’s User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Some optional features can be unlocked for a single run by choosing to watch a rewarded ad; no feature ever requires this.

Buying Folio Pro removes ads entirely.

4Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Folio Pro is sold as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or handle your card, bank or address details. The app receives only whether a valid purchase exists for the Google account signed in on the device.

5Scanning and text recognition

The document scanner and the text recognition used by OCR are provided by Google Play services (ML Kit). The recognition models are downloaded once by Play services and then run on your device. The pages you scan and the images you process are not uploaded for recognition.

6The Web to PDF tool

If you use the Web → PDF tool, the app fetches the web page at the address you type. That request goes to that website, exactly as it would if you opened the page in a browser, and that site’s own privacy policy applies to it. The request does not pass through us.

7Permissions the app requests, and why

PermissionWhy
Internet, network stateAds, purchases, the one-time text-recognition model download, and the Web → PDF tool. Every other tool works with no connection at all.
BillingGoogle Play purchases.
Advertising IDUsed by the AdMob SDK to select and measure ads. Not used by the app itself.
NotificationsProgress and completion notices for a job you started, so a long document can finish after you leave the screen.
Foreground service (data sync)Lets a running job finish in the background instead of being killed when you switch apps.
Read storage (Android 10 and below only)Lets the Files tab’s “On this phone” list show the PDFs already on your device. Requested from that screen, never at launch.

Folio does not request your contacts, location, microphone, call logs or SMS, and it does not request all-files access. On Android 11 and above there is no permission that would let it see other apps’ documents, and we deliberately do not ask for the one that exists: the “On this phone” list there shows the PDFs Folio itself created, and everything else is opened through the system picker. Apart from that, the app sees only the files you hand it through the picker, a share, or “Open with”.

8Sharing and export

When you share or export a document, Android hands it to the app you pick. What happens to it then is governed by that app’s privacy policy, not ours. Printing goes through Android’s own print system.

9Children

Folio is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

10Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, deletion and portability apply to personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to return, correct or erase. In practice this means:

  • Ads: buy Pro to remove them, or change your consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android’s own settings.
  • Recent files: remove entries one at a time in the app, or clear the app’s data to remove all of them.
  • Everything else: uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Purchases: managed in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

11Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Data on your device is kept until you delete it, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Google retains purchase and advertising records under its own policies.

12Security

Because your documents never leave your device, the main protection is Android’s own: app sandboxing, and the device encryption that applies when you set a screen lock. Files Folio writes to Documents/Folio are ordinary files in your shared storage and are readable by other apps you grant storage access to, as any saved document would be.

When you password-protect a PDF with Folio, the encryption is applied to the file itself using AES-256. We cannot recover that password for you — we never receive it.
