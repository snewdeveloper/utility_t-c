2Training safely

Boxing, MMA, Muay Thai, kickboxing, grappling and high-intensity interval training are physically demanding and carry a risk of injury. Rounds only keeps time; it does not coach you, assess your fitness, or know your health.

  • Check with a doctor before starting or intensifying a training programme, especially if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, an injury, or are pregnant.
  • Choose round, rest and workout lengths that suit your condition. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or shortness of breath.
  • Use proper equipment and supervision for contact work and sparring.

You train at your own risk. The presets in the app reflect common formats for each sport; they are not a recommendation that they are right for you.

3A training timer, not an official clock

Rounds is designed to keep accurate time with the screen off and other apps open, but a phone is not a certified timing device. Bells can be delayed or silenced by things outside the app’s control: the phone’s volume or Do Not Disturb settings, battery saving that stops background apps, a low battery, or another app taking the audio.

Do not rely on Rounds as the sole or official clock for a sanctioned bout, competition, grading or any situation where a timing error could cause harm or affect a result.
