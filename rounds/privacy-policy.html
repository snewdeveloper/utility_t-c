Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026  ·  Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what the Rounds Android app, a round timer for boxing, MMA, Muay Thai and other combat training, does with your information. It describes what the app actually does, not the widest set of things a policy could permit. Every claim below corresponds to something in the app’s source.

Rounds is published by SkIndie Dev (“we”, “us”), Tamil Nadu, India. Contact: snewdeveloper@gmail.com

At a glance

  • Your training history stays on your phone. Your workouts, sessions, streaks and goals are stored on the device. We do not upload them, and we cannot see them.
  • There is no Rounds server and no account. No sign-up, no login, no user profile. We do not know who you are.
  • We collect nothing about you. The app ships no analytics provider and no crash reporter.
  • No location, no health sensors, no microphone. Rounds is a timer; it does not track where you are or measure your body.
  • Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. Where consent is required, the app asks before requesting any ad.
  • We do not sell your data. There is no data to sell.

1Information stored on your device

Everything in this section stays on your phone. None of it is transmitted to us.

WhatDetails
Custom workoutsThe workouts you build: name, number of rounds, round lengths, rest, warning and prep times.

You can delete custom workouts in the app. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app removes all of it, subject to Android backup (Section 6). None of it is stored anywhere we can reach.

2Information we collect about you

None. Nothing is sent to us, because there is nowhere to send it: Rounds ships no analytics provider and no crash reporter, and we operate no server.

The app records a small set of anonymous events — a workout started or finished, the upgrade screen was shown, a purchase completed — in Android’s on-device system log. They contain no personal information, are not sent anywhere by Rounds, and are overwritten by the system as the log fills.

If this ever changes, for example by adding a crash reporter, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before the release that changes it.

3Advertising (Google AdMob)

Rounds is free, and the free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, and occasionally a full-screen ad after you finish a workout. Ads are never shown on the timer while a workout is running, never after your first workout, and at most once a day full-screen.

AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and coarse device and usage data to select and measure ads. On recent Android versions this can include the Privacy Sandbox ad services that Android itself provides. That processing is Google’s, under Google’s privacy policy; we receive no personal data from it. Your training history is never shared with AdMob.

Where consent is legally required, the app asks for it through Google’s User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. Where that applies, you can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy choices in the app.

Buying Rounds Pro removes ads entirely.

4Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Rounds Pro is sold as a one-time lifetime purchase or a yearly subscription. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or handle your card, bank or address details. The app receives only whether a valid purchase exists for the Google account signed in on the device.

5Sounds and voice callouts

Bells, buzzers and countdown beeps are generated inside the app; nothing is downloaded. Voice callouts (“Round three”, “Ten seconds”) are spoken by the text-to-speech engine you have chosen in Android’s settings. The app gives that engine only those fixed phrases. The engine is provided by your phone’s maker or by Google, and its own privacy terms apply to it.

Rounds never records audio and does not request the microphone.

6Android backup

If you have turned on backup in Android’s settings, Android may include the app’s data — your custom workouts, training history and settings — in your device backup to your own Google account, and restore it when you set up a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under your account; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in Android’s backup settings.

8Notifications, widget and Quick Settings tile

While a workout runs, the notification, the home-screen widget and the Quick Settings tile show the workout’s name, the current round and the clock. These appear on your lock screen and home screen, so anyone who can see your phone can see them. The widget and tile read this from the app on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

9Sharing and export

  • Sharing a workout creates a short text containing the workout’s name and timings, and Android hands it to the app you pick. It contains nothing about you or your history. What happens to it then is governed by that app’s privacy policy.
  • Opening a shared workout reads the timings from the link and shows them to you before anything is saved.
  • Exporting history (Rounds Pro) writes a CSV file of your sessions to a location you choose through Android’s file picker. Once saved, it is an ordinary file under your control.

10Children

Rounds is a general-audience training tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

11Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, deletion and portability apply to personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to return, correct or erase. In practice this means:

  • Ads: buy Pro to remove them, or change your consent from Ad privacy choices where it applies. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android’s own settings.
  • Workouts and history: delete custom workouts in the app. To remove your training history, clear the app’s data in Android’s settings, which removes everything. Pro users can export history as CSV first.
  • Backups: managed in Android’s backup settings for your Google account.
  • Purchases: managed in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

12Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Data on your device is kept until you delete it, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Google retains purchase, advertising and backup records under its own policies.

13Security

Because your training data never leaves your device through us, the main protection is Android’s own: app sandboxing, and the device encryption that applies when you set a screen lock. A history file you export is an ordinary file, readable by any app you give access to the folder you save it in.
